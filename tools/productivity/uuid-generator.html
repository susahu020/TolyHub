What is a UUID?

A UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit label used to uniquely identify information in computer systems. UUID v4 is randomly generated, making collisions practically impossible. Format: xxxxxxxx-xxxx-4xxx-yxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx
