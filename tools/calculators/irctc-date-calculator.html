⚠️
Tatkal booking opens exactly one day before the journey date, counted from the train's originating station — not your boarding station. Agents cannot book during the first 30 minutes of the window (10:00–10:30 AM for AC, 11:00–11:30 AM for non-AC). Aadhaar-based OTP authentication is mandatory for online Tatkal bookings.
