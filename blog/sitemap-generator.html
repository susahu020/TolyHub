Why Sitemaps Help SEO

While not a ranking factor directly, sitemaps help search engines find pages that might otherwise be missed, especially on large sites, sites with weak internal linking, or newly published pages awaiting their first crawl.

How to Use the Sitemap Generator

Enter your site's URLs (one per line, or generate from a list), and the tool produces a properly formatted XML sitemap file following the sitemaps.org protocol, ready to upload to your site root.

After Generating Your Sitemap

Upload sitemap.xml to your site's root directory, reference it in your robots.txt file, and submit it directly through Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools for faster discovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many URLs can a sitemap contain?

A single sitemap file can list up to 50,000 URLs; larger sites use a sitemap index file referencing multiple sitemap files.

Do I need to resubmit my sitemap after every update?

Search engines periodically recrawl sitemaps automatically, but resubmitting after major changes can prompt faster recrawling.
