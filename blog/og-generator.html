When a page is shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, or other platforms, Open Graph tags control the title, description, and image shown in the preview card.

Why Open Graph Tags Matter

Without proper Open Graph (OG) tags, social platforms may pull an arbitrary image or the wrong text when your page is shared, hurting click-through rates and brand presentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need separate tags for Twitter/X?

X (Twitter) supports Open Graph tags as a fallback, but dedicated Twitter Card tags offer more control — consider adding both.

How do I test if my OG tags work?

Use Facebook's Sharing Debugger or LinkedIn's Post Inspector to preview and refresh how your link appears.
