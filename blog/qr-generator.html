Common QR Code Uses

Businesses use QR codes on packaging, business cards, posters, and menus to link directly to websites, contact details, payment pages, or Wi-Fi credentials without typing.

How to Use the QR Generator

Enter the text or URL you want the QR code to encode, and the tool instantly generates a downloadable QR code image that can be scanned by any smartphone camera.

Design Tips

Ensure sufficient contrast between the QR code and its background, leave white space (a 'quiet zone') around it, and test scanning from the intended print size before mass-producing materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do QR codes expire?

Static QR codes (like those generated here) never expire, but if they link to a URL and that URL is taken down, the code will no longer work.

What's the maximum amount of text a QR code can hold?

QR codes can encode a few thousand characters, but shorter content (like a URL) produces a simpler, more scannable code.
