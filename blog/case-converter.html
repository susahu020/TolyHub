Frequently Asked Questions

What is Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of most words (typically excluding small words like 'a', 'the', 'of', 'and') — commonly used for headlines and titles.

What is camelCase used for?

camelCase joins words together with no spaces, capitalizing the first letter of each word except the first — e.g. 'myVariableName'. It's a common naming convention in programming languages like JavaScript and Java.

Will converting case affect punctuation or numbers?

No, punctuation, numbers, and symbols remain unchanged — only the letter casing is converted.

Can I undo a conversion?

Simply paste your original text again, or use your browser's undo (Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z) inside the text box before converting.
