Frequently Asked Questions

Does the calculator account for different month lengths?

Yes, the calculation correctly handles months with different numbers of days (28-31) as well as leap years when computing date differences or adding/subtracting days.

Can I subtract days to get a past date?

Yes, entering a negative number of days (or selecting 'subtract') will calculate a date that falls before the starting date.

Does the date difference include both the start and end date?

Typically, the difference is calculated as the number of days between the two dates (exclusive of one endpoint), which matches how most 'days until' calculations work — check the result against your specific use case if exact inclusive/exclusive counting matters.

Can I calculate differences in weeks, months, or years instead of just days?

If the tool provides this breakdown, the total day difference can also be expressed in larger units — otherwise, you can convert manually (e.g., divide days by 7 for weeks).
