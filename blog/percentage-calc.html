Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Percentage increase is calculated as ((new value - old value) / old value) × 100 — our calculator does this automatically when you select the percentage change option and enter both values.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100 — for example, 30 is what percentage of 120 is calculated as (30 / 120) × 100 = 25%.

How do I calculate X% of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100 — for example, 20% of 150 is (150 × 20) / 100 = 30.

Can I calculate percentage decrease the same way as increase?

Yes, the formula is the same — ((new value - old value) / old value) × 100 — a negative result indicates a percentage decrease.
