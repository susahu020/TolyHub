🏦 EMI Calculator: Calculate Your Monthly Loan Installments

Before taking a loan, understanding your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) helps you budget and compare loan offers. This calculator computes EMI instantly from loan amount, interest rate, and tenure.

🛠 Try it now: EMI Calculator — free, no login required.

How EMI Is Calculated

EMI is calculated using the loan principal, the annual interest rate, and the loan tenure (in months), using a standard amortization formula that ensures equal monthly payments covering both principal and interest over the loan term.

How to Use the EMI Calculator

Enter your loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan tenure in months or years. The tool instantly calculates your monthly EMI, along with the total interest payable and total repayment amount.

Using This for Loan Comparisons

Run the calculation with different interest rates or tenures to see how they affect your monthly payment and total interest — useful when comparing offers from multiple lenders before committing to a loan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this account for processing fees?

No, this calculates the EMI based on principal, rate, and tenure only — processing fees and other charges should be considered separately.

What happens if I make a prepayment?

This calculator shows the standard EMI schedule; prepayments would reduce the outstanding principal and could shorten the tenure or reduce future EMIs, which would require recalculation.
