Why Exact Age Calculation Is Tricky

Simply subtracting birth year from the current year ignores whether the birthday has occurred yet this year, and doesn't account for the exact number of months and days, which can matter for eligibility requirements or legal purposes.

How to Use the Age Calculator

Common Uses
