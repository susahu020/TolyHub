Where Character Limits Apply

Google typically truncates meta titles around 60 characters and meta descriptions around 155-160 characters. Social platforms like X limit posts to 280 characters. SMS messages are often capped at 160 characters per segment.

How to Use This Tool

Type or paste your text and watch the live character count update, both with and without spaces. This makes it easy to trim copy until it fits perfectly within your target limit.

Best Practices

When writing meta titles and descriptions, front-load your primary keyword and keep your most important message within the first 50-60 characters, since that's what's most likely to display in search results.
