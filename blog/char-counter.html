Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between characters with and without spaces?

Characters with spaces counts every character including blank spaces between words. Characters without spaces excludes all whitespace, which is useful when a platform's limit specifically excludes spaces.

Does it count emojis and special characters?

Yes, emojis and special characters are counted, though note that some platforms (like SMS and Twitter) may count certain emojis as 2 characters due to how they're encoded — always double-check against the platform's own counter for critical limits.

Can I count characters in multiple languages?

Yes, the tool works with any Unicode text, including non-Latin scripts like Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, and Japanese.

Is there a limit to how much text I can paste?

No artificial limit is imposed by the tool, though very large documents (tens of thousands of words) may take a moment longer to process in some browsers.
