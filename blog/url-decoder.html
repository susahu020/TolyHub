Frequently Asked Questions

What does %20 mean?

%20 is the percent-encoded representation of a space character. Other common codes include %3D for '=', %26 for '&', and %3F for '?'.

Can I decode an entire URL, including the domain?

Yes, you can paste a full URL — only the percent-encoded portions will be converted, while the rest of the URL remains unchanged.

What if the encoded text contains a '+' instead of '%20'?

In some encoding contexts (like form submissions), spaces are represented as '+' rather than '%20' — if your decoded result still shows '+' where you expect spaces, that's the encoding convention used by the source.

Is decoding URLs safe?

Yes, decoding simply reveals the underlying text — it doesn't visit or execute the URL. All decoding happens locally in your browser.
