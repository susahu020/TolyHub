Why Preview Your SERP Snippet

Google often truncates titles and descriptions that exceed certain pixel widths, not just character counts. A visual preview helps you craft compelling, complete snippets that won't get cut off awkwardly.

How to Use the SERP Preview Tool

Enter your page's title, URL, and meta description. The tool renders a live preview mimicking Google's search result appearance on both desktop and mobile.

Writing Better Snippets

Include your target keyword naturally near the beginning of both the title and description, and write descriptions that include a clear value proposition or call to action to improve click-through rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this guarantee my title will appear exactly as previewed?

Google sometimes rewrites titles and descriptions based on the query and page content, so this preview shows the intended appearance, not a guarantee.

Does it check character limits?

Yes, it visually indicates when your title or description is likely to be truncated.
