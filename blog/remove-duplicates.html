Frequently Asked Questions

Is the duplicate check case-sensitive?

By default, the comparison looks at the exact text of each line, so 'Apple' and 'apple' may be treated as different lines unless your input is already normalized to a consistent case — you can run your list through the Case Converter first if needed.

Does it remove duplicates based on the whole line or individual words?

The tool compares entire lines, not individual words — so two lines must match completely (including spacing) to be considered duplicates.

Will the order of my list change?

No, the tool preserves the original order, keeping the first occurrence of each unique line and removing any later repeats.

Can I undo the operation?

Yes, simply paste your original list again if you want to start over before removing duplicates.
