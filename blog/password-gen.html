Why Strong Passwords Matter

Short or predictable passwords can be cracked through brute-force or dictionary attacks in minutes. Long, random passwords combining uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols are exponentially harder to crack.

How to Use the Password Generator

Choose your desired password length and which character types to include (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols), then generate a new random password instantly. Use the copy button to copy it to your clipboard.

Best Practices

Use a unique password for every account, aim for at least 16 characters where allowed, and store passwords in a reputable password manager rather than reusing or memorizing them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are generated passwords stored anywhere?

No, passwords are generated locally in your browser and are never transmitted or stored.

How long should my password be?

At least 12-16 characters is generally recommended; longer is better, especially for important accounts.
