🧾 GST Calculator: Calculate GST-Inclusive and Exclusive Amounts

Goods and Services Tax (GST) calculations are a daily necessity for businesses and consumers in countries that use GST. This calculator handles both adding and removing GST.

Adding vs. Removing GST

Adding GST means calculating the final price including tax from a pre-tax amount. Removing GST (reverse calculation) means determining the original pre-tax amount from a GST-inclusive total — both are common needs.

How to Use the GST Calculator

Enter the amount, select the applicable GST rate, and choose whether you're adding GST to a base amount or extracting the base amount from a GST-inclusive total. The tool instantly shows the breakdown.

Why Accuracy Matters

Incorrect GST calculations on invoices can cause compliance issues and accounting discrepancies, so a reliable quick-reference calculator is valuable for freelancers, small businesses, and shoppers alike.

Frequently Asked Questions
