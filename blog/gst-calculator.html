Frequently Asked Questions

How is GST calculated when adding to a price?

GST amount = base price × (GST rate / 100). The total price is the base price plus the GST amount — for example, ₹1,000 with 18% GST adds ₹180, for a total of ₹1,180.

How do I find the GST amount from a GST-inclusive price?

Base price = GST-inclusive price / (1 + GST rate/100), and GST amount = GST-inclusive price - base price — for example, ₹1,180 at 18% GST has a base price of ₹1,000 and GST of ₹180.

What are the common GST rate slabs in India?

Common GST rates include 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%, depending on the category of goods or services — always confirm the applicable rate for your specific product or service.

Is this calculator suitable for filing official tax returns?

This tool is intended for quick estimates and general calculations. For official tax filings and compliance, consult a qualified tax professional or your accounting software, as rates and rules can vary and change.
