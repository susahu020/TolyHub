How Strength Is Evaluated

Password strength checkers typically assess length, character variety (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols), and patterns like common words, sequences, or repeated characters that make passwords easier to guess.

How to Use This Tool

Type your password into the input field. The tool analyzes it in real time and displays a strength rating along with specific suggestions for improvement.

Improving Weak Passwords

If your password is rated weak, try increasing its length first — length has the biggest impact on cracking resistance — then add variety in character types and avoid dictionary words or personal information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my password sent anywhere when I check it?

No, the analysis happens entirely in your browser — your password is never transmitted or logged.

Does a 'strong' rating mean the password can't be cracked?

No password is uncrackable given enough time and resources, but a strong rating indicates significant resistance to common automated attacks.
