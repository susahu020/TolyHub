About TolyHub

TolyHub is a free collection of 30+ browser-based tools for SEO, development, security, productivity and everyday calculations — built so anyone can get things done without signing up, paying, or installing anything.

Our Mission

Most "free" online tools come with a catch — a forced account, a daily limit, a watermark on your output, or a pile of intrusive ads. We built TolyHub to be different. Every tool runs directly in your browser using client-side JavaScript, which means your data never leaves your device and there's nothing to sign up for.

What You'll Find Here

  • SEO Tools — meta tag generator, sitemap generator, robots.txt generator, schema markup generator, SERP preview, and Open Graph tag generator.
  • Developer Tools — JSON formatter/validator/minifier, Base64 encoder/decoder, URL encoder/decoder, and MD5 hash generator.
  • Text Tools — word counter, character counter, case converter, text comparison, sort and duplicate-removal utilities.
  • Security Tools — password generator, password strength checker, and SHA-256 hash generator.
  • Productivity Tools — QR code generator and UUID generator.
  • Calculators — percentage, age, date difference, GST, and EMI calculators.

Why It's Free

Because every tool runs locally in your browser, our running costs are minimal. We keep the site sustainable through unobtrusive advertising, which lets us offer every tool completely free, with no usage limits and no account required — and we intend to keep it that way.

Privacy First

We don't ask for your files, your text, or your personal information. Calculations and conversions happen on your device — nothing is uploaded to a server unless a specific tool explicitly says so. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Questions or Feedback?

We're constantly adding new tools and improving existing ones. If you have a suggestion, found a bug, or just want to say hello, visit our Contact page — we'd love to hear from you.
